Latency: source results retain actual `processing_seconds`. Document what interval was measured (extraction worker only, full upload, complete intake, retries, human review). Do not substitute the configured timeout for observed latency. A local text/PDF fixture does not measure live speech latency.

Cost: use actual Bolna execution billing and direct Sarvam usage/billing for that isolated session. Token counts are not a cost figure without verified billing/pricing. Include failed attempts when computing cost per completed intake. State whether hosting, storage, taxes, network, human time and retries are excluded. This implementation did not perform new live calls or generate a measured provider cost per completed intake.

Suggested observation questions: “How much do you think remains?”, “Was that money received or only promised?”, “What will you do next?”, “Has a complaint actually been submitted?”, “What will happen with this reminder?”, and “Does this upload prove the sender’s statement?” Capture misunderstandings and corrections without recording unnecessary personal information.
